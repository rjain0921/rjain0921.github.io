Research

Where the two fields meet

Current research direction as a PhD scholar in Computer Science & Engineering at MNNIT Allahabad.

3D Computer Vision

Understanding a scene the way a body moving through space does: recovering depth, geometry and structure from images and point clouds, and turning flat pixels back into a three-dimensional world a system can reason about.

Radiology AI

Applying that same spatial reasoning to medical imaging — CT, MRI and X-ray volumes are already 3D data. The open question is how to help a model read them with the structural awareness a radiologist brings to a scan.

Following an M.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering (CGPA 9.01) from J.K. Institute of Applied Physics and Technology, University of Allahabad, this is the direction my PhD work is now built around.
